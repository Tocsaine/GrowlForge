# GrowlForge 2.2.2 — Nolly Reference Preset Bank

This update expands the embedded factory bank and organizes it into three practical groups:

- **Pre-Amp** — place GrowlForge before Archetype Nolly X.
- **Post-Amp** — place GrowlForge after the Nolly amp and cabinet.
- **Creative** — less conventional combinations for sound design and destructive processing.

The preset browser now uses category submenus. User presets remain in their own submenu.

## Reference chain

The album-inspired presets were designed around the **Archetype Nolly X Rhythm amp** shown in the reference screenshot.

Suggested neutral starting point:

- Nolly input and output at unity;
- Nolly gate and overdrive/boost disabled;
- doubler disabled;
- Rhythm amp gain around 4–5;
- amp EQ, resonance and presence near noon;
- a balanced Nolly cabinet preset with no extreme post-EQ.

Pre-Amp presets shape the DI before Nolly. Post-Amp presets shape the complete amp-and-cab result.
