# GrowlForge 2.0.3 — Drive DC and subsonic fix

The Drive asymmetric transfer previously returned a large non-zero value for a zero input.
At high Drive this produced a strong DC component that spectrum analyzers displayed at the
lowest bins, often appearing as energy around 10 Hz.

The fix has two parts:

1. The exact zero-input response of the existing asymmetric transfer is subtracted before
   the oversampling reconstruction filters. This preserves the nonlinear curve while removing
   its constant output bias.
2. A Drive-only 20 Hz second-order Butterworth high-pass removes residual signal-dependent
   DC and subsonic energy generated by asymmetric clipping.

The high-pass is reset and bypassed when Drive is zero. Parameter IDs, state format, GUI, x2
behavior, and all non-Drive controls are unchanged.
