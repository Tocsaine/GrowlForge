GrowlForge 2.0.3 verification
================================

Build
- Portable CMake/Ninja Release build: PASS

Drive silence output
- Sample rates tested: 44.1, 48, 96 and 192 kHz
- Drive values tested: 0.1, 1.0, 5.0 and 10.0
- Worst absolute output for a zero input: 0.0

48 kHz signal checks at Drive 10
- Zero input: mean 0.0, RMS 0.0, maximum 0.0
- 10 Hz sine: mean approximately 2.9e-11
- 82 Hz sine: mean approximately 6e-12
- 440 Hz sine: mean approximately 1.1e-10

Compatibility
- With Drive at 0, a two-second render using Growl, Grind, Fuzz, Mass,
  Bite, Bloom, Sag, Dynamics, Texture, Attack and Compression was byte-for-byte
  identical to GrowlForge 2.0.2.
